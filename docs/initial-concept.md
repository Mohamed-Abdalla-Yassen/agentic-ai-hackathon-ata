A booking app for centers and workspaces. that uses AI for matching users with the best options based on their needs and preferences. The app allows users to search for available workspaces/centers, view detailed information about each option.

## Features

- workspace/center can register an account and input their data, such as rooms, availability, and pricing. and can also update their information as needed.
- room data includes concrete attributes such as capacity, price, has projector, has whiteboard, etc. and includes a description and images of the workspace/center.
- data also includes text data such as notes for other information that don't fit into the concrete attributes. such as that the place is typically busy at certain times, or that it is a good place for certain types of events.
- users can search for workspaces/centers based on their needs, such as location, capacity, price range, and available amenities. and can also include a note for additional information that may help the AI match them with the best options.
- it's not realistic to expect all workspace/centers to use the app, so there should be a way for a user (that might be a moderator/admin) to add a workspace/center to the listing, although some information such as availability might not be possible to show. and the listing must include some contact information for the workspace/center, such as a phone number or email address, so that users can reach out to them directly. 
- If a workspace/center that wasn't using the app started using the app, then the app should allow them to claim their listing and update their information accordingly.
